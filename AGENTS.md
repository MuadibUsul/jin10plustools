# Project requirements

- As requested on 2026-10-15, the unified workspace is live at `plus.tlines.tech/`, with daily selection at `/daily/` and fixed briefs at `/briefs/`. `/test/` is retired and its services are stopped. Use the existing production database and storage; do not replace them with the former test snapshot or recreate the test site unless explicitly requested.
- For 固定快讯, Plus新鲜事 and Plus注解辅助, all final image downloads (including future AI-generated images) must use `jin10-server/app/services/image_delivery.py`.
- Use the unmodified original `app/assets/plus-watermark.png` at the lower right, scaled proportionally. Final files must be at most 266,000 bytes. Optimize compression before reducing dimensions; preserve readable text and chart lines. Do not bypass a clarity/size failure with an oversized or illegible download.
- Download filenames are `YYYYMMDD（MD5(title UTF-8)）` plus the actual image extension. The date is the issue's production date. Keep internal original data and image snapshots for reproducible rendering.
- All fixed explanatory notes must remain editable and versioned. Category defaults are team-scoped. Valuation, turnover and 美股晴雨表 notes are drawn on the delivered image (requested 2026-10-17); their body text stays dynamic-only. Retain body placement for other categories until requested otherwise. The 原油ETF closing note is appended inline at the end of the body, never as a separate line (requested 2026-10-19).
- The market valuation composite chart was approved on 2026-10-15 and is frozen. Preserve its equal-sized aligned panels, matching typography and axes styling, Buffett 0.5–1.3 reference scale with 1.0/0.7/0.5 level labels, editable image notes, original watermark and 266,000-byte delivery limit. Change this chart only on a new explicit user request. As requested on 2026-10-17, the lower ERP panel's CSI 300 left axis fits the observed index range (500-point ticks, round bottom with ≥250 headroom) instead of a fixed 0 bottom; keep that behavior.
- Plus新鲜事 and Plus注解辅助 are currently placeholders. These rules govern their future implementation; do not claim their generation workflows already exist.
- As requested on 2026-10-17, all stored business data must be structured in the database and exportable by time range through 设置 → 数据库. New tables belong in the `app/services/data_export.py` registry so they appear in the export panel automatically. As requested on 2026-10-19, the export panel is available to every signed-in account (still strictly team-scoped); admin-only checks remain only for AI keys and scheduling. Never export password hashes, encrypted keys, raw capture HTML or source-snapshot binaries.
- As requested on 2026-10-19, a cached brief product may never outlive the rules that produced it. Every generated payload records `pipeline_signature()` and every delivered image cache keys on `delivery_signature()`; a cached body/image is reused only when those signatures match. Never reintroduce a date-only product cache or a hand-bumped render version as the only gate.
- As requested on 2026-10-19, the fixed-brief AI engine is selectable by editors and admins between DeepSeek, the chat2api node and the official OpenAI API; API keys and scheduling remain admin-only (for models the node cannot reach). The rule draft always remains; AI only chooses approved wording and never changes numbers, and switching providers must re-run the optional AI instead of reusing the previous provider's candidate. The chat2api node silently maps unknown models to gpt-4o: validate and record the effective model, and never claim an unsupported requested model.
- As requested on 2026-10-20, pruning may keep at most two recent versions plus the confirmed version; never delete a confirmed edition. Viewers see the confirmed edition, and editors can switch between the retained versions.
- As requested on 2026-10-19, chat2api login credentials are managed only in 设置 → chat2api (admin-only); the chat2api port is host-local, public token-management paths are blocked, and the OpenAI-compatible API stays under the random `/API_PREFIX` path.
- As requested on 2026-10-19, the 美股市场结构 body's first item opens with the previous trading day's Nasdaq, S&P 500 and Dow close changes (e.g. 前一个交易日纳斯达克指数收跌2.15%、标普500指数收跌1.09%、道琼斯指数收跌0.34%). Recompute these from every fetch; never store fixed percentages in the template.
- As requested on 2026-10-20, the five fixed-brief production flow must run through the Skill copy stage. The validated rule copy remains the only source of numbers, charts and snapshots; AI may only polish wording through the team-selected provider and must never change/add/drop numbers, add promotional/investment text, or publish. If AI is unconfigured, out of quota, times out or fails validation, the job automatically falls back to the complete fixed-combination rule copy and still succeeds. The generic News Skill OS pages and `news_*` persistence APIs remain a default-off branch capability until separately deployed.
